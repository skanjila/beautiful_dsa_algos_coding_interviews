# MLE Role Variants

Not all MAANG-style MLE interviews emphasize the same things.

## Meta / feed-heavy product ML style

Likely emphasis:

- ranking and recommendation funnels
- online experimentation
- exploration and long-term feedback loops
- large-scale feature pipelines
- integrity and abuse-aware ranking

## Google / search and large-scale retrieval style

Likely emphasis:

- search ranking
- retrieval and relevance metrics
- ads or marketplace prediction systems
- latency-aware ML infrastructure
- evaluation rigor

## Apple / search, quality, privacy, and low-latency product ML style

Likely emphasis:

- search and assistant quality
- ranking and query understanding
- evaluation data and failure analysis
- low-latency serving
- privacy and on-device or hybrid constraints

## Amazon / practical production MLE style

Likely emphasis:

- recommendation and demand or conversion systems
- production tradeoffs
- experimentation and business metrics
- data pipeline ownership
- cost-aware serving and operational robustness

## Netflix / recommendation and experimentation style

Likely emphasis:

- recommendation quality
- long-term engagement metrics
- experimentation and causal thinking
- content and personalization signals
- platform and batch-serving tradeoffs

## How To Use This

Do not memorize company stereotypes. Use them to bias your prep time:

- if the role sounds search-heavy, rehearse retrieval and ranking
- if it sounds assistant or GenAI-heavy, rehearse evaluation and low-latency AI systems
- if it sounds platform-heavy, rehearse training-serving parity, monitoring, and feature stores
- if it sounds product-heavy, rehearse experimentation, exploration, and long-term metrics
